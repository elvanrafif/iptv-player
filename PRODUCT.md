# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Non-technical family members sitting on the couch at night, lights dimmed, looking for a live TV channel to watch together. They open the app on a tablet or laptop. Sessions are lean-back: pick a channel, watch, occasionally browse. Speed and clarity matter more than power-user density.

## Product Purpose

A personal IPTV player for Indonesian households. Loads M3U playlists, browses channels by group, tracks favorites and watch history, and plays HLS streams inline. Success means finding and playing a channel in under 10 seconds.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, calm, trusted. Not a developer tool. Not a branded product. A refined appliance.

## Anti-references

- YouTube / Netflix: no algorithm cards, no thumbnails-as-hero, no recommendation grids
- Android TV / Kodi: no oversized remote-friendly tiles or cluttered plugin panels
- Generic SaaS dark dashboards: no sidebar-plus-cards admin-panel aesthetic
- Current design's blue-navy + hot-red palette: cliché generic dark app, no connection to a real TV experience

## Design Principles

1. **The screen is the room.** Dark surfaces should feel warm and cinematic, not developer-tool cold.
2. **Playing is the point.** The video player is never buried or secondary; it anchors the layout.
3. **One action at a time.** Casual users shouldn't feel overwhelmed. Navigation is quiet; content is loud.
4. **Legibility over density.** Larger touch targets, readable channel names, no micro-text.
5. **Speed over animation.** Smooth but not showy. Transitions serve orientation, not spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Large tap targets (minimum 44px)
- High contrast text on dark surfaces
- Reduced motion preference respected
